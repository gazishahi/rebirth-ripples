# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-25)

First release, for the Steam version of FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH, update 1.004.

### Ocean
- Wind-driven FFT ocean with whitecaps, sea-state presets and per-pixel wave detail.
- Swimming, floating objects and the Tiny Bronco follow the simulated waves.

### Wakes and splashes
- Wakes, bow waves and entry rings, with drifting foam.
- A swimming Chocobo's chest collar of white water.
- Splashes and ripples from strokes and limbs.
- Weapon splashes and the game's own splash effects on impact.

### Rivers and lakes
- Wakes, ripples and a current that carries you.

### Characters
- Characters wade in shallow water instead of clipping through the seabed.
- Wet clothes, skin and fur, drying over time.

### Release features
- In-game settings panel (Ctrl+Shift+F1) with live tuning, save and quality presets.
- Game-version check: the mod switches itself off on an unknown game version.
- With ShaderInjector installed, the water shaders are built on your PC from your own ShaderInjector settings.
